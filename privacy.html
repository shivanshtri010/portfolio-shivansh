Privacy / Analytics

Privacy, without the fine print.

This portfolio is a static site. It has no accounts, forms or checkout. Analytics is optional, loads only after you allow it, and is used to understand which sections and projects are useful.

What is measured

When analytics is allowed, the following may be recorded:

  • Page views, referral source, approximate country, device type, browser and operating system.
  • Which sections were viewed, which case studies were opened, and which outbound links were used.
  • Resume downloads and contact-link interactions.
  • A masked session replay of interaction patterns, with input values and marked contact details hidden.

PostHog is the primary tool. Cloudflare Web Analytics may be used for an additional, cookieless traffic summary. Both are configured to avoid advertising profiles and cross-site tracking.

What is not collected

  • No passwords, payment information or account credentials.
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-site behavioural profiles.
  • No attempt to identify an individual visitor. Anonymous visitors are not turned into identifiable person profiles.
  • No raw visitor IP logs are accessed by this site. GitHub Pages, as the host, may keep infrastructure logs under its own policies.

Your choice

Analytics stays off until you choose “Allow analytics”. Declining is remembered in local storage on this device. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured automatically. You can change your choice at any time using the button below; the page will reload with analytics disabled until you opt in again.

Providers

Read the provider policies for details on processing and retention: PostHog privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy. The portfolio remains fully usable if either service is blocked.
